Frequently asked questions

Is SkillArena free?

Yes, browsing and saving events is free for participants.

How do I post an event?

Create an account, choose Post event, and fill in the title, date, cost, and registration link.

What does the Verified badge mean?

The organizer confirmed the event details. New events start as pending until verified.

Where is my data stored?

In this prototype, in your own browser only. Nothing is sent to a server.
